# GrowthIQ cinematic product film

## User correction and scope
The user rejected both the movie-to-HTML presentation and the subsequent product walkthrough. Their latest direction is cinematic animation on the desktop keynote stage; mobile work is explicitly out of scope. This revision is a short silent product film with camera choreography, layered product imagery and a handoff into the working application. It is not a feature tour, a six-minute slide sequence or a live AI demonstration.

Mode: Experience. Visual authority: actual GrowthIQ product screens and local Source Serif/Source Sans families. A navy stage derives from the product's Advisory primary. The movie supplies only the narrative principle: signal → business significance → focused question. This is the user-directed replacement of the presentation layer, not a global brand change.

THESIS: Pull meaning out of the product interface, then physically carry it toward the next action.
FIRST VIEWPORT: A captured GrowthIQ briefing enters in perspective as a real React Three Fiber camera approaches it. The product occupies the stage; compact playback controls sit at the edge.
SIGNATURE: At 3.6 seconds the Sika signal lifts from its projected location in the screen into crisp HTML. At 10 seconds it recedes while the business implication comes forward. At 15 seconds the camera travels across the 3D scene toward the actual Ask screen, with a connecting path. At 19 seconds the matching question comes forward. At 22 seconds motion stops and Continue in the product becomes available.
FORM: A single 22-second timeline; no chapter pages, large presenter bar, persistent explanatory card grid or decorative market network. The lifted signal, reader and composer preserve recognizable product typography, surfaces and data. Foreground copy is HTML for crispness; background product screens are locally captured image textures.

## Playback
Autoplay once when renderer loads; stop at22 seconds. Play/Pause, previous/next shot and a scrubbable playhead remain visible. Space toggles playback outside interactive controls; arrows/Page Up/Page Down select shots; Home/R rewinds; End goes to the final shot. Fullscreen and Exit film are explicit controls. No audio. Reduced motion uses manually selected still compositions rather than autoplay. WebGL/loading failure offers a product handoff.

React Three Fiber renders on demand at capped DPR1–1.75; the camera and foreground projections share one timeline. Two local image textures and one short linking line are the only scene assets. Foreground typography is enlarged for the stage. Desktop validation targets1280×720,1440×900 and1920×1080. No mobile-specific design or QA is planned for this revision. Actual projector, GPU and clicker hardware remain untested.
