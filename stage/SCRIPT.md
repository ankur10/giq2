# GrowthIQ on stage: presenter script

About 4 minutes 45 seconds. One press of the clicker per line below. All companies and figures are fictional.

**Open:** `stage.html` in a browser, press **F** for fullscreen. Runs offline.

| Key | Action |
| --- | --- |
| Clicker, → or space | Next step |
| ← | Previous step |
| ] and [ | Next / previous act |
| 1–7 | Jump to an act |
| R | Restart |
| F | Fullscreen |

## Cold open

| # | On screen | You say |
| --- | --- | --- |
| 1 | A clock: 6:42, Tuesday. | "This is Maya. She runs strategy at Norvane, a company that has made pumps for a hundred years." |
| 2 | One notification from GrowthIQ Radar. | "This is how her Tuesday starts." |

## Act 1: It finds you

| # | On screen | You say |
| --- | --- | --- |
| 3 | The Radar digest opens. | "Three things moved overnight. She didn't go looking; it found her." |
| 4 | One story is picked out: Kestrow Group acquires Thalic Cooling. | "One of them is a rival buying a liquid-cooling start-up." |
| 5 | The headline becomes the signal in Your Briefing. | "In GrowthIQ that isn't just news." |
| 6 | "What this means for Norvane". | "It tells her why it matters to her company: AI data centres need liquid cooling, and liquid cooling runs on pumps." |

## Act 2: What's really going on?

| # | On screen | You say |
| --- | --- | --- |
| 7 | Markets: Norvane's ecosystem. | "So what's behind the headline? Here's Norvane's world." |
| 8 | The ecosystem opens into a field of connected markets; a path lights up. | "A need that starts in AI data centres travels, through cooling, to exactly what Norvane makes." |
| 9 | Data-centre liquid cooling: growth and size. | "Here's that market: growing about six times faster than her core business." |
| 10 | Competitors: who is present where. | "Here's who is already in it. Kestrow, as of this week. Norvane isn't." |
| 11 | Benchmarking. | "And here's how those rivals compare with us." |

## Act 3: Ask

| # | On screen | You say |
| --- | --- | --- |
| 12 | The question is typed. | "So she asks what she'd ask her smartest analyst." |
| 13 | The answer writes itself. | (Pause. Let it build.) "Yes, as a component supplier first." |
| 14 | The evidence docks beneath it. | "With the evidence attached: everything we just looked at." |
| 15 | Deep Research: a six-chapter scope. | "And if she wants the full study, the scope is already drafted." |

## Act 4: Go deeper

| # | On screen | You say |
| --- | --- | --- |
| 16 | One line is highlighted: the open question. | "The answer is honest about what it doesn't know." |
| 17 | The question goes to a domain expert and is sent. | "Some questions need a person. One click, and it's with an expert." |
| 18 | Customers filter down to Arden Compute. | "And it isn't theoretical: one of our own customers is already building data centres." |

## Act 5: Make it real

| # | On screen | You say |
| --- | --- | --- |
| 19 | Intelligence Studio: three starting points. | "Now she turns thinking into something she can hand over." |
| 20 | The market-model brief and what it delivers. | "Her question and context come with her." |
| 21 | Three documents build. | "A market model. A profile of Kestrow. A market-entry strategy." |
| 22 | They stack into a board pack. | "By nine o'clock she has what used to take weeks." |

## Close

| # | On screen | You say |
| --- | --- | --- |
| 23 | A Radar tracker is switched on. | "And she asks it to keep watching." |
| 24 | Every screen of the morning, at once. | "One morning. One decision." |
| 25 | "We see growth before it happens." | (Say nothing. Let the line land.) |

## If time is short

Skip act 2 or act 4 with **]**. The story still holds: the answer in act 3 states the market and the competition itself.
