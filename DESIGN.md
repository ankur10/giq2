# Design System: GrowthIQ Briefing

## Overview

**Creative North Star: "The Intelligence Briefing"**

GrowthIQ uses one editorial visual system with three shortlisted options: Precision, Advisory and Mineral, in that order. Advisory is the default, pairing white reading surfaces with ink navy and precise blue. Grove, Midnight, Evergreen, Graphite, Slate, Atelier and Parchment were retired following user feedback. Precision uses stronger sans-serif interface headings and more compact control rhythm while retaining serif article reading. Advisory and Mineral preserve their typography and component rhythm; every option preserves layout breakpoints, data and behavior. Source Serif 4 gives intelligence a considered editorial voice; Source Sans 3 keeps operations clear; JetBrains Mono identifies compact measurements. The product remains an operating workspace with readable evidence and contextual actions.

This is the implemented replacement direction documented from local source evidence. The approved direction does not constitute visual approval of the implementation. Earlier declarations remain in styles.css; this document follows the later Briefing cascade, its responsive overrides and the semantic palette roles in themes.css. Palette values are mirrored in themes.json.

**Key Characteristics:**
- Editorial serif titles and reading copy paired with practical sans-serif controls.
- Theme-aware surfaces and fine borders organize information with minimal elevation.
- Adjacent selection and context keep the evidence near the next action.

### Neutral
- **Canvas, surface, sunken and nav:** overall workspace, reading surfaces, contextual panels and navigation.
- **Ink / muted:** main and supporting copy.
- **Line / control-border:** group separators and input boundaries. Control boundaries use their stronger dedicated role.

**The Theme Role Rule.** Apply each palette through semantic roles, including foregrounds, focus, hover, disabled, validation and data marks; do not substitute raw color literals or identify a theme by its swatch alone.

**The Evidence Rule.** Use existing records and honest availability states; visual polish must not imply new data or completed services.
