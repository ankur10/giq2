What every agent produces

Every agent output has the same six parts, shown in GrowthIQ, never as a block of text. Every call also carries a confidence level.

PartWhat it is
What changedOpens every output after the first run: what moved since the previous run, shown on the view itself. ChatGPT has no previous run to compare with.
The callA verdict, ranking or recommendation in one line, beside the agent's signature view.
The evidenceThe sources and assumptions behind it, each one openable.
Next actionsButtons that start the next agent, ask an expert, or send the result up to leadership as a Stakeholder Brief or a bet in the Growth Portfolio.
Traceable figuresA number appears only if it comes from MnM data, a disclosed fact, or a count of observed events, and every view names its source. Judgements such as relevance, fit or likelihood are shown as ratings with their evidence, never as a precise number or a score.
A watchThe conditions that would change the call, set as alerts. Offered only where something could overturn it.

Why this is not ChatGPT

A capable analyst with ChatGPT and a web browser could produce a text version of most of these agents in an hour. Every agent must therefore pass a simple test: it fails that comparison for at least two of the five reasons below. The test is about substance; the signature views are how the difference becomes visible.

Only GrowthIQ hasWhat it meansWhy ChatGPT can't match it
MnM numbersProprietary forecasts, segment splits, connected markets and company coverage.It only has what is on the public web.
HistoryEvery run is kept, so each output shows what changed since the last one; MnM's own forecast revisions add a longer view.Each conversation starts from nothing.
MemoryThe client's markets, key customers, competitors, criteria and open bets, built up over months.It doesn't know the client unless told every time.
Always onAgents re-run, re-rank and alert without being asked.It answers once and stops.
PeopleMnM analysts who can confirm or overturn a call, and every figure traced to a specific MnM table.No one stands behind its numbers.

Monitoring: the engine behind Watch, Tracker and Radar

Commercial web-monitoring services can run scheduled searches and watch web pages for change. GrowthIQ users cannot use them directly, and the raw output is a stream of links and diffs. GrowthIQ turns that stream into calls. The same layer powers the Tracker and Radar products and the alerts behind Watch.

ServiceWhat it doesBest for
Parallel MonitorRuns a plain-language query on a schedule (hourly, daily or weekly) and returns only new items, with duplicates removed. Results arrive by webhook as structured data with source links.Finding new events about a company, market or topic
Exa MonitorsRuns a search on a schedule (one hour minimum) and returns only new results. The output can follow a schema you set, with citations and a confidence level kept separately. Runs can be triggered manually and their history read back.Discovery where structured data is needed
Context.dev MonitorsWatches given pages, sitemaps or a site's key pages and reports what changed, with a diff, a summary and evidence. Compares exact text or meaning. Does not discover new news. Credit-based, with 1,000 free credits a month.Competitors' and customers' own sites: product, pricing and careers pages, new pages

Engagement and email

Leadership pays for GrowthIQ but rarely logs in, so email is how GrowthIQ reaches the person who renews. For strategy and market intelligence teams, a useful daily or weekly email turns occasional use into a habit. The risk is noise: monitors produce many events, and if each one becomes an email, users learn to ignore GrowthIQ. Every email must be worth opening.

PrincipleIn practice
The email is the productThe call, the reason and the evidence are in the email itself, so a reader can act without logging in. Only the detail sits behind a link.
Silent unless it mattersNo email when nothing important happened. A short email is fine; an empty or padded one never is.
Act in one clickAdd to the Growth Portfolio, dismiss, mark as not relevant, send to SalesPlay, or forward as a Stakeholder Brief. Each click also teaches the ranking what this person cares about.
Built to be forwardedA brief forwarded from an analyst to their manager is how GrowthIQ reaches new people inside a client, and new users are how accounts grow.
Matched to the roleAnalysts get a daily feed; leadership gets a weekly summary. Neither gets the other's email.
