# GrowthIQ Briefing · approved direction

## Scope
Operate and Read, all 15 existing prototype routes. The user selected Briefing and approved Axiom-inspired typography, mineral surfaces, contextual interactions and existing data only. No backend/API changes. Code-led build, continuing the existing approved prototype workflow.

## First viewport
A quiet light navigation rail, a serif page title and an open briefing workspace. A compact selected-signal index leads into a large editorial reader. No promotional hero, fabricated KPI or decorative cards. The reading pane shows existing summary, business relevance, source availability and next actions.

## Signature interaction
Select → read → inspect context → save locally or carry the signal into a research question. The list remains alongside the reader. Market selections show adjacent facts. Competitor selection changes comparison columns; differences mode isolates differing captured values.

## Visual world
Axiom reference: Source Serif 4, Source Sans 3, JetBrains Mono for measurement. Mineral gray canvas, white reading surface, charcoal primary controls and restrained blue selected states. Sharp 2–3px controls and consistent spacing.

## Motion
Only reader title/summary changes have a 240ms visible entrance; controls use 180ms feedback. Reduced-motion preference disables transitions and animation.

## Product constraints
No Axiom market data imported. Existing GrowthIQ data.js is unchanged. Missing source URLs, full reports and generated answers remain unavailable; do not simulate server success. Local read, saved, query and draft states are browser-only.

## Verification
Inspect all routes at desktop and mobile. Exercise selection, saved/unread filtering, research context, market search/selection, difference comparison and financial row selection. Preserve customer access boundary and support service boundaries.
