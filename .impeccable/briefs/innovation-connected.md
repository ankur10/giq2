# Connected Opportunity Brief

## Direction contract

User-approved direction: rebuild Innovation OS around connected opportunity reasoning and one continuous opportunity brief with contextual editors. Preserve the previous prototype and existing GrowthIQ for comparison. This replaces the prototype's information architecture while extending the existing Advisory visual system; it is not a new brand.

Mode: Operate, with a read-first brief. Persona: a VP of Innovation at fictional Meridian Specialty Materials. The opportunity is the persistent unit of work. Home points to the next unresolved action; Portfolio and the brief derive from the same browser-local store.

## First viewport

Home leads with Battery Materials Expansion, the capacity signal and its conditional effect on the scenario, alongside the two questions a validation investment must answer. The brief leads with the thesis, strategic-fit hypothesis and strongest objection. A compact section index anchors into one document; no separate editable-form pages or permanent third column. Preserve bundled Source Serif / Source Sans and existing semantic theme tokens.

## Signature interaction

Follow the chain from captured battery-production context through the fictional customer signal, application hypothesis and capability hypothesis to the qualification constraint. Each link opens its source and implication. Contextual editors preserve the reading location, support nested source/challenge drilldown with Back, and retain uncommitted drafts. The announced capacity and the assumed conversion into materials volume are explicitly distinct.

## Decision and motion grammar

Reviews require an intentional assessment and rationale. Decisions require an explicit outcome; funding requires review of validation criteria and unresolved evidence. Receipts confirm the recorded state, focus moves to the result, and earlier snapshots remain immutable after working edits. Short drawer and receipt transitions explain changes; reduced-motion mode removes them. No ambient animation or simulated AI thinking.
